About Us

Built on Craftmanship. Driven by Exellence

Who We Are

Soweto City Electrical Wholesalers is a premier provider of electrical solutions, serving businesses, municipalities, utilities and governments across Gauteng and beyond. Established in Soweto, we specialize in innovative and reliable electrical solutions that drive economic development and sustainability.

Our company collaborates with a vast network of world-class partners and experienced professionals to deliver high-quality turnkey solutions, including electrical supplies, component manufacturing and solar energy systems.

Our Founder

Saniboy Alfred Molatlhoe

Driven by a desire to build a better life, he went on to study to become an electrician. He trained at Dube Technical Vocational College and began his career as an electrician for the City of Johannesburg.

From a single electrical appliance wholesaler in Soweto, he expanded the business into a multi-branch electrical solutions provider, surviving economic downturns and setbacks such as the COVID-19 pandemic and looting incidents. Today, his company remains a beacon of black entrepreneurship and innovation, manufacturing electrical components and expanding operations through strategic partnerships.

Our Mission

To provide cost-effective, clean, and modern energy solutions that enhance sustainability while reducing electricity costs. We are dedicated to delivering fast, safe, and reliable electrical solutions; educating customers on product safety and optimal use; adhering to industry standards and safety regulations; and providing seamless customer experiences through convenience and reliability.

Our Vision

To become the premier provider of electrical and energy solutions in South Africa, driving economic development and sustainability through innovative and reliable products and services, while expanding nationally through franchising and distribution.
